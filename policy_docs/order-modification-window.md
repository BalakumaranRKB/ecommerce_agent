# Order Modification and Cancellation Window

An order can be modified or cancelled by the customer without any charge within
**60 minutes** of being placed, provided it has not yet entered the packing
stage. After that window, changes to the shipping address are possible only
while the order status is `in_transit`, and the carrier charges a **$4.50**
address-correction fee. Once an order is marked `shipped`, it cannot be
cancelled; the customer must wait for delivery and then start a return.
Cancellations approved before dispatch are refunded in full within **3 business
days**.
