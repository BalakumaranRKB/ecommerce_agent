# Shipping Delay Compensation

An order is considered delayed when it arrives more than **3 days** past its
promised delivery date. Delays of **4 to 9 days** entitle the customer to a
store credit worth **10%** of the order value. Delays of **10 days or more**
entitle the customer to a full refund of shipping charges plus a **20%** store
credit. If the carrier has not scanned the package for **14 consecutive days**,
the order is treated as lost and a replacement is dispatched at no charge.
Store credit is applied to the customer account within **24 hours** of approval
and does not expire.
