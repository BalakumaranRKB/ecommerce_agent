# Subscription Cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same calendar day each month. A
customer may cancel at any time from the account settings page, and the
cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so access
continues until then. If a cancellation is requested within **48 hours** of a
renewal charge, that charge is refunded in full. Outside that window, the
monthly charge is non-refundable, but a customer who cancels within the first
**14 days** of their very first subscription month is entitled to a full refund
of that first charge. Paused subscriptions resume automatically after **3
months** unless cancelled.
