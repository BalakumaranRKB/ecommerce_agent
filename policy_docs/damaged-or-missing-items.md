# Damaged or Missing Items

Damage or missing items must be reported within **7 days** of the delivery
date. The customer is asked to supply at least **2 photographs** of the
packaging and the affected item. Once a report is accepted, a replacement is
dispatched within **2 business days** at no cost, and the damaged item does not
need to be returned when its value is under **$50**. Items valued at **$50 or
more** require the damaged unit to be returned using a prepaid label before the
replacement ships. If a replacement is unavailable, a full refund is issued
within **5 business days**.
