# Account Suspension and Appeals

Accounts are flagged automatically after **3 disputed chargebacks** within a
rolling **12-month** period, and are suspended after **5**. A flagged account
can still place orders but is limited to **2 open orders** at a time. A
suspended account cannot place new orders. Customers may appeal a suspension in
writing within **30 days** of the suspension notice; appeals are reviewed by
the trust and safety team within **5 business days**. If an appeal succeeds,
the account is restored to active standing immediately and any store credit is
reinstated. A second suspension after a successful appeal is permanent and
cannot be appealed again.
