# Refund Eligibility and Window

Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of the delivery date. Refunds
are issued to the original payment method and appear within **5 to 7 business
days** after the returned item is received at our warehouse. Unopened items are
refunded in full. Opened electronics are subject to a **15% restocking fee**,
which is deducted from the refund amount. Orders with a status of `cancelled`
that were never shipped are refunded in full within **3 business days**, with no
return required. Requests made more than 30 days after delivery are not eligible
for a refund and must be escalated to a human supervisor for a goodwill review.
